# DishIt — Project Proposal

## Overview

DishIt is a social recipe and meal-sharing platform that lets users discover, rate, and share home-cooked dishes with their community. Think of it as the intersection of a recipe app and a social feed — where every meal tells a story.

---

## The Problem

Home cooks have no dedicated space to share what they're actually making day-to-day. Existing platforms are either too polished (food blogs, YouTube) or too noisy (general social media). DishIt gives everyday cooks a focused place to post, discover, and connect around real food.

---

## The Solution

A lightweight web app where users can:
- Post a dish with a photo, ingredients, and a short method
- Browse a feed of dishes from people they follow
- Rate and comment on dishes
- Search by ingredient, cuisine, or dietary tag
